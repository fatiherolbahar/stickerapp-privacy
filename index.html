Privacy Policy

Sticker App · Last updated: October 2026
In short: Sticker App does not collect, track, or share any personal data. All processing occurs 100% locally on your device.

1. What the App Does

Sticker App lets you create custom stickers from photos, GIFs, and videos, organize them into packs, and export them directly to WhatsApp.

2. Information We Collect

None. The app contains no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no trackers. We do not transmit any data to our servers or third parties.

3. Photos and Videos

Media is selected via the native iOS photo picker. All image processing—including background removal and outlines—happens entirely on-device.

4. Data Stored on Device

Your sticker packs and settings are stored exclusively in your device's local sandbox. Uninstalling the app permanently removes them.

5. Permissions

Photos: Used solely to import selected media and save generated stickers to your photo library.

6. WhatsApp Export

Stickers are transferred locally via the system pasteboard to WhatsApp. No data passes through our servers.

7. Third-Party Code

Open-source components (such as WebP encoding) execute offline on your device and collect no telemetry.

8. Children's Privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to Policy

Any revisions to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with an updated timestamp.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub Issues repository.
